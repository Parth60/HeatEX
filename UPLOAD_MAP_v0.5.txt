WHAT v0.5 DOES:
  - pure or multicomponent streams
  - mole-basis or mass-basis input
  - automatic basis conversion and normalization
  - enthalpy-based heat duty
  - temperature-dependent properties
  - PR cubic EOS
  - SRK cubic EOS
  - NRTL activity coefficients
  - UNIQUAC activity coefficients
  - JSON-driven interaction parameters
  - bubble/dew/phase screening
  - Z-factor diagnostics
  - T–Q profile
  - minimum approach temperature
  - optimizer remains connected to the same simulator

IMPORTANT:
  NRTL/UNIQUAC/PR/SRK predictions require appropriate validated interaction
  parameters for serious design work.

  v0.5 does NOT yet claim rigorous phase-change design. If a phase boundary is
  detected the program warns you. Condenser/reboiler/two-phase calculations are v0.6.
